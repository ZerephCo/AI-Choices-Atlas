## 1. What it is
A **native macOS terminal purpose-built for AI coding agents**, built on **Ghostty**. It adds **vertical tabs and notifications** and is "built for multitasking, organization, and programmability" — i.e. a terminal cockpit for running and organizing many agent sessions at once. Very large early traction (~25k ⭐).
