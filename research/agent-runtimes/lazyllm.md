## 1. What it is
A **low-code framework for building multi-agent LLM applications**: compose **data flows** that wire models, RAG, tools, and agents together, and move from **prototype to deployment** with minimal boilerplate.

- **Low-code / minimal boilerplate.**
- **Python**, Apache-2.0.
