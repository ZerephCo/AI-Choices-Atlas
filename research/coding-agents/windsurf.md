## 1. What it is
A **proprietary AI-native IDE** (a VS Code–based editor) built around the **Cascade** agent — multi-file edits, terminal actions, and tool use — plus fast **Tab** autocomplete. Formerly **Codeium**. It qualifies for this atlas via a **permanent free tier that needs no credit card** (verified 2026-07).

## 2. Access (why it's in the atlas)
- **Permanent free plan**, no credit card: unlimited Tab autocomplete + a monthly prompt-credit allowance (~25 credits) for premium-model use.
- Paid Pro/Teams unlock more usage (the credit allowance is limited for sustained daily work).

## 3. Features
- **Cascade agent** (multi-file, terminal, tools) in an AI-native IDE.
- **Unlimited Tab autocomplete** (free plan).
- Plugins for other editors; enterprise options.

## 4. Choose / avoid
- **Choose it when** you want a polished proprietary agentic IDE with a real no-CC free tier.
- **Avoid when** you need open/local tooling (Continue), or heavy daily use beyond the free credits.
- Compare with Cursor (§1.2, also free-tier) and open Continue/Void (§1.2).
