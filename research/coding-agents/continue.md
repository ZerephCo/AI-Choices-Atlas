## 1. What it is
An **open-source coding agent** delivered as a **VS Code** extension, a **JetBrains** plugin, and a **CLI** — with autocomplete, chat, edit, and agent modes. Its defining trait is being **model-agnostic**: point it at local models (Ollama, LM Studio) for a fully-local, zero-cost setup, or at any cloud provider.

- **Customizable** rules and context; Apache-2.0.
